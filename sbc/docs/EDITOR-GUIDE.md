# How to update the SBC website — a guide for editors

You need your phone and the email address the foundation gave you. Nothing to install.

## 1. Save the editor on your phone (once)

1. Open the editor link the foundation sent you. It looks like **https://(the website)/admin/**
2. **iPhone:** tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
   **Android:** tap the ⋮ menu, then **Add to Home screen**.
3. You now have an “SBC Editor” icon. Tap it any time.

## 2. Sign in

**With a password:** type your email address and your password, then tap **Sign in**. Tap **Show** to check what you typed.

**With an emailed link (no password needed):** type your email address and tap **Email me a sign-in link**. Open the email on the same phone and tap **Sign in**.

**The link opened in the wrong app, or it says it has expired?** Go back to the editor, request a new link, and type the **6-digit code** from the email into the box under “Link opens somewhere else?”, then tap **Sign in with the code**.

**Forgot your password?** Tap **Forgot your password?**, type your email address and tap **Email me a reset link**. Open the email, tap the link, and choose a new password.

**To set or change your password:** on the main screen tap **Set or change my password** (at the bottom), type a new password (at least 8 characters) twice and tap **Save**.

## 3. The main screen

The big green button, **Add an update**, is for the most common job. Under it are plain buttons for everything else:

- **Edit an update**
- **Edit or add a programme**
- **Edit or add to the photo gallery**
- **Edit the About page**
- **Edit bank details** *(only if you are the owner)*
- **Edit contact details**

At the bottom: **Look at the website** (opens it in a new tab) and **Sign out**.

## 4. Add an update with photos

1. Tap **Add an update**.
2. **Title** — a short headline, e.g. “Rice reached 40 families”.
3. **What happened?** — a few sentences. Leave a blank line between paragraphs.
4. **Programme** — choose the programme, or **General update**.
5. Tap **Add photos** and pick **several photos at once** from your camera or photo library. Use **◀ ▶** to change the order and **✕** to take one out. The first photo is shown first.
6. Tap **Save** — once. When you see **Saved. Your changes are live.**, it is on the website. Tap **See it on the website** to look.

Saving *is* publishing. There is no extra step.

## 5. Fix an update

1. Tap **Edit an update**, then the update.
2. Change anything and tap **Save**. It keeps its original date and place in the list.
3. To take a wrong update off the website: open it, scroll down, tap **Remove from website**, then **Yes, remove it**.

## 6. Edit or add a programme

1. Tap **Edit or add a programme**. Every programme is a button — tap the one you want. **＋ Add a programme** is at the bottom.
2. You can change: **Programme name**, **Short name for the menu**, **Where it happens**, **Date**, **Caption under the main photo**, **Description**. Anything marked *(optional)* can be left empty. **Date** shows on the website next to the place; leave it empty if the programme is ongoing.
3. **Photos:** tap **Add photos** and pick as many as you like at once (up to 40). The **first photo is the main photo**; use **◀ ▶** to move another photo to the front. These photos also appear on the **Gallery** page under the programme's name.
4. **Videos:**
   - Tap **Add a video** and pick one or more clips (MP4, MOV or WebM, up to **50 MB** each). Keep the page open while they upload; you can tap **✕** to stop one.
   - **Or paste a YouTube link** into the box (any ordinary YouTube link works). Visitors tap the picture to play it.
   - If a video is refused as too big, record a shorter clip, or on an iPhone use Settings → Camera → Record Video → **720p HD**. For videos to play for everyone, use Settings → Camera → Formats → **Most Compatible**.
5. Tap **Save**.

## 7. The photo gallery

Tap **Edit or add to the photo gallery**. These are photos that belong to no particular programme.

1. Tap **＋ Add photos** and pick several at once.
2. Use **←** and **→** to put them in order. The first is **Shown first**. Tap **✕** to take one out.
3. Tap **Save**. On the website's Gallery page these appear first, under **More photos**.

## 8. The About page

1. Tap **Edit the About page** and change the words you want.
2. **Who carries the work out** shows a card for each group. For each group you can add a **photo** (optional), change its **name** and **what they do**. Tap **Remove this group**, then **Yes, remove it**, to take one away. **＋ Add a group** adds a new one. Groups appear on the page in this order.
3. Tap **Save**.

## 9. Contact details

Tap **Edit contact details**. An email, phone number or address only appears on the website if you type something in it — leave a box empty to keep it off the website. Tap **Save**.

## 10. Bank details (only for the owner)

If you are the owner you will see **Edit bank details**. Fill in **Name on the account**, **Bank**, **Branch** and **Account number** (and a **SWIFT code** for gifts from abroad, and a **Note for donors** if you want one). **Check the account number twice** — everyone sees it on the Give page and donors copy it exactly. Tap **Save**.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| *Your changes weren’t saved. Check your connection and try again.* | Nothing is lost — your text and photos are still on the screen. Check your signal and tap **Save** again. |
| A field is highlighted in red | Read the message under it, fix it, tap **Save**. |
| *Someone else changed this after you opened it.* | Tap **Reload** to see their version, then make your change again. |
| *You’ve been signed out.* | Tap **Email me a sign-in link**, sign in, come back to the screen and tap **Save** again. |
| *That password isn’t right.* | Check for typing mistakes (tap **Show**), or tap **Email me a sign-in link**, or **Forgot your password?** |
| *Not set up yet* after signing in | The foundation has not added your email yet. Ask them. |
| A photo is refused | Choose a normal photo (JPG or PNG). On an iPhone, Settings → Camera → Formats → **Most Compatible** helps. |
| A video is refused | It is probably over 50 MB or not MP4/MOV/WebM. Choose a shorter clip or record at 720p. |

**Need help?** Contact the foundation (ask them to put a name and phone number here).
