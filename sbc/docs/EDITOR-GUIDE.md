# How to update the SBC website — a guide for editors

You need your phone and the email address the foundation gave you. Nothing to install.

## 1. Save the editor on your phone (once)

1. Open the editor link the foundation sent you. It looks like **https://(the website)/admin/**
2. **iPhone:** tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
   **Android:** tap the ⋮ menu, then **Add to Home screen**.
3. You now have an “SBC Editor” icon. Tap it any time.

## 2. Sign in

1. Type your email address and tap **Email me a sign-in link**.
2. Open your email on the same phone and tap **Sign in**. You are in.
3. You stay signed in on that phone. If it asks again, repeat these steps.

**Prefer a password?** Type your email address and your password under **Or sign in with a password**, then tap **Sign in with password**. To set one: after signing in, tap **Set or change my password** on the main screen, type a new password (at least 8 characters) twice and tap **Save**. The emailed link keeps working too.

**The link opened in the wrong app, or it says it has expired?**
Go back to the editor, request a new link, and this time type the **6-digit code** from the email into the box under “Link opens somewhere else?”, then tap **Sign in with the code**.

## 3. Add an update with photos

1. Tap **Add an update**.
2. **Title** — a short headline, e.g. “Rice reached 40 families”.
3. **What happened?** — write a few sentences. Leave a blank line between paragraphs.
4. **Programme** — choose the programme it belongs to, or **General update** if it is general news.
5. Tap **Add photos** and choose from your camera or photo library. Small pictures appear.
   - You can choose **many photos at once**: tap them all in the photo picker, then confirm.
   - The first photo is shown first. Use **◀ ▶** to move a photo, **✕** to take one out.
6. Tap **Save** — once. When you see **Saved. Your changes are live.**, it is on the website. Tap **See it on the website** to look.

Saving *is* publishing. There is no extra step.

## 4. Fix an update

1. Tap **Edit an update**.
2. Tap the update. Change anything you like, then tap **Save**.
   The update keeps its original date and place in the list.
3. To take a wrong update off the website: open it, scroll down and tap **Remove from website**, then **Yes, remove it**.

## 5. Edit a programme

1. Tap **Edit a programme**. Every programme is a button: tap the one you want.
2. You can change: **Programme name**, **Short name for the menu**, **Where it happens**, **Caption under the main photo**, **Description**, and the photos. Anything marked *(optional)* can be left empty.
3. The **first photo is the main photo**. To change it, move another photo to the front with **◀**.
4. Tap **Add photos** and choose **as many photos as you like at once** (up to 40 per programme). They also appear on the website's **Gallery** page under that programme's name.
5. Under **Videos**, tap **Add videos** to add short clips (MP4, MOV or WebM, up to **50 MB** each). A still picture is made for each, and visitors press play to watch — nothing plays by itself. Use **◀ ▶** to reorder and **✕** to remove.
   *If a video is refused as too big:* record a shorter clip, or on an iPhone use Settings → Camera → Record Video → **720p HD**. For videos to play for everyone, record them in the most compatible format (iPhone: Settings → Camera → Formats → **Most Compatible**).
6. Tap **Save**. (Videos take a little longer; the button shows the progress.)
7. To add a new programme, tap **Add a programme** at the bottom of the list, fill it in and **Save**. It appears after the others.

## 6. Change the About page or contact details

1. Tap **Edit the About page**.
2. Change the words you want. Under **Contact details (optional)**, an email, phone or address only appears on the website once you type something in it. Leave it empty to keep it hidden.
3. **Who carries the work out** shows a card for each group on the About page. Tap **＋ Add a group** for a new one, give it a **Name of the group**, say **What they do**, and add a photo with **Add photo**. To take a group off the page tap **Delete this group**, then **Yes, delete it**. (Nothing changes on the website until you tap **Save**.)
4. Tap **Save**.

## 7. Bank details (only for the owner)

If you are the owner of the website you will also see **Edit bank details** on the main screen. Fill in **Account name**, **Bank**, **Account number** and the rest, **check every number carefully** (everyone sees them on the Give page), and tap **Save**. Other editors do not see this screen.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| *Your changes weren’t saved. Check your connection and try again.* | Nothing is lost — your text and photos are still on the screen. Check your signal and tap **Save** again. |
| A field is highlighted in red | Read the message under it, fix it, tap **Save**. |
| *Someone else changed this after you opened it.* | Tap **Reload** to see their version, then make your change again. |
| *You’ve been signed out.* | Tap **Email me a sign-in link**, sign in, come back to the screen and tap **Save** again. |
| *Not set up yet* after signing in | The foundation has not added your email yet. Ask them. |
| A photo is refused | Choose a normal photo (JPG or PNG). On an iPhone, Settings → Camera → Formats → **Most Compatible** helps. |
| A video is refused | It is probably over 50 MB or not MP4/MOV/WebM. Choose a shorter clip or record at 720p. |
| *That email or password isn’t right.* | Check for typing mistakes, or tap **Email me a sign-in link** instead. |

**Need help?** Contact the foundation (ask them to put a name and phone number here).
